Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CameoCraft Talent ("we," "us," "our") handles information in connection with this website, cameocraftinc.com (the "Site"). CameoCraft Talent is based in the United States, and we work with comic cons, fan conventions, and event organizers around the world. We built this Site to be as simple as possible: we do not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers of any kind.

Information We Collect

We do not use contact forms, newsletters, or account sign-ups on this Site. The only way to reach us is directly, through the phone number and email addresses shown on the Site — and we only receive the information you choose to include when you call or email us (for example, your name, contact details, and the content of your message).

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records standard technical information for security and performance purposes, such as IP address, browser type, and pages visited. We do not use this information to identify individual visitors.

Cookies & Tracking

This Site does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising, or social media tracking tools. If that ever changes, we will update this policy and add a cookie notice accordingly.

How We Use Information

Any information you send us directly (by phone or email) is used solely to respond to your inquiry — for example, to discuss a talent booking. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

Images of Talent

Photos of celebrities and public figures featured on this Site are used for informational and promotional purposes only, in connection with our talent booking services. All such images remain the property of their respective owners. If you are a rights holder and have a concern about an image on this Site, please contact us and we will address it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information we hold about you, and to request that it be corrected or deleted. Since we only hold information you've sent us directly, you can exercise these rights at any time by contacting us using the details below.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the UK, or another jurisdiction with similar data protection laws, you additionally have the right to request access to, rectification of, or erasure of your data; to restrict or object to our processing of it; to receive a copy of it in a portable format; and to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority. Because CameoCraft Talent is based in the United States, any information you send us is stored and processed in the United States.

International Visitors

We work with talent bookings, comic cons, and fan conventions worldwide, so this Site is accessed by visitors from many countries. Wherever you're contacting us from, the same rules in this policy apply: we don't collect personal data automatically, and we only use what you choose to send us directly to respond to your inquiry.

Children's Privacy

This Site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
